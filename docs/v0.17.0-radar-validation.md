# v0.17.0 — Automotive Radar Detection Simulation

**Module:** `sensor_sim/radar.py` · **Demo:** `examples/radar_demo.py`
**Tests:** `tests/test_radar.py` (+20, total 168 passing)

## Problem (the missing RF sensor in the ADAS suite)

The simulator produces IMU, GNSS, wheel odometry, latency/time-sync,
display-time prediction, uncertainty, outages, ADAS marker projection,
visibility/occlusion, hazard arbitration, LiDAR point clouds (v0.12.0) and
camera optical flow (v0.13.0).  But the sensor that actually *drives* ACC /
FCW / AEB target lists on production vehicles -- the **automotive radar** --
was missing.  Radar is complementary to the optical sensors in a way that
matters for fusion development: it measures range and *radial velocity*
directly (Doppler), with near-zero sensitivity to lighting, but it is
structurally blind to cross-range motion.  This module adds a forward-looking
77 GHz radar that turns analytic world objects into a sparse detection list:
range / azimuth / elevation + Doppler range-rate, gated by FOV, range and a
radar-equation detection probability.

## Design

| Piece | Role |
|---|---|
| `RadarConfig` | FOV half-spans (az `±60°`, el `±10°`), range gate `[1, 200] m`, per-axis Gaussian noise (range 0.1 m / angle 0.5° / range-rate 0.15 m/s), radar-equation reference point (`rcs_ref=10 m²` @ `range_ref=50 m` @ `snr_ref=40 dB`) and a linear detection threshold. |
| `RadarFrame` | Sparse per-scan detection list as parallel arrays: measured `ranges / range_rates / azimuths / elevations / points / snr_db / object_ids` plus `range_true / range_rate_true` truth channels for validation. |
| `RadarSensor` | Rigid body mount + `_quat_to_rotmat` plumbing (same frames as `LidarSensor` / `CameraSensor`). `scan()` iterates world `Object`s, gates bearing → FOV and surface range → range gate, then draws detection from `P_d = SNR/(SNR+thresh)`. |

Physics encoded:

- **Range to the near surface.**  The range is the analytic raycast
  intersection with the object along the centre bearing (reusing the LiDAR
  primitives' `intersect()`), so an extended 4.4 m lead vehicle centred 60 m
  ahead reads ~57.8 m -- a resolved target reads closer than its centre, like
  a real radar.
- **Radar equation.**  `SNR = snr_ref · (rcs/rcs_ref) · (range_ref/R)⁴`, with
  RCS from an explicit `rcs_sqm` attribute when present, else mapped from the
  LiDAR `reflectivity` (full reflector = `rcs_ref`).  Detection probability is
  the saturating curve `P_d = SNR/(SNR + detect_threshold)`: ~0.99 at the
  reference point, ~0.86 at 2× range, ~0.28 at 4× range.
- **Doppler, closing positive.**  `v_r = (v_sensor − v_obj)·u` along the
  centre bearing; positive = closing (the ACC convention).  Translation-only
  kinematics; mount rotation and target micro-Doppler are documented as out of
  scope.
- **No clutter layer.**  `GroundPlane` is skipped: this is the object-target
  detection layer, matching how the ADAS stack consumes radar targets.

| Stationary geometry | Ego + target at rest, 60 m apart | range-rate = 0 exactly |

| Extended-target range | Box centred 60 m, half-length 2.2 m | r = 57.8 m (surface, not centre) |

| Range gate | 3 m / 50 m / 400 m targets | only the 50 m target detected |
| Doppler tracking | 1 s convoy run at 10 m/s closing | measured range drops exactly 10 m; every scan v_r = 10 m/s |

| Doppler-noise MC | σ = 0.3 m/s, 3000 scans | std in [0.22, 0.42] |

## Production traps surfaced (why this matters for FCW fusion)

1. **Crossing targets are Doppler-invisible.**  In the demo a pedestrian
   walking 2.5 m/s laterally across the ego path sweeps ~28° of azimuth while
   its *measured range-rate changes by < 4 m/s* -- the reading is dominated by
   the ego's own ~25 m/s closing on the pedestrian's down-range position.  A
   radar-only FCW cannot distinguish a crossing pedestrian from a stopped
   one until the target is nearly abeam; the lateral component must come from
   camera (or a tracker fusing both).  This is the standard fusion argument,
   now reproducible in-sim.
2. **Doppler sign encodes geometry, not intent.**  +10 m/s means "range
   shrinking at 10 m/s" whether the lead is slower ahead or the ego is
   accelerating into a stationary object -- arbitration (v0.11 hazard layer)
   must combine range-rate with range and TTC, not react to the sign alone.
3. **RCS matters more than distance for detection.**  At 4× reference range
   a 10 m² car still detects at P_d ≈ 0.28 while a 1 m² pedestrian at the
   same range is nearly invisible (SNR ∝ RCS·R⁻⁴).  Target-list dropouts are
   therefore *range- and class-dependent*; fusion filters must not assume a
   constant measurement rate.

## Scope notes

Out of scope by design (documented in the module docstring): ground/static
clutter returns, one-detection-per-object (no glint/multipath ghosts,
no range-Doppler cell quantization), mount-rotation Doppler and target
micro-Doppler.  These are the natural next layer if a radar *raw-detection*
(or clutter) simulator is ever needed.
